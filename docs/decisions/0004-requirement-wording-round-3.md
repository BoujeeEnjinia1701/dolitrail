# 0004: Wording of R7 and R8

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** accepted. Decided by Amish on 2026-10-04: "For round 3, I agree with all your proposed recommendations". For DoliTrail this accepts option A of open decision O5 in DLT-DEC-001 (portfolio item 1A).

## Context

Decisions 8A and 9A (DLT-DDR-003) met R7 and R8 with two bags and four people, while the requirement text still said one person carries the kit and two bearers lift at steps.

## Options considered

A: restate R7 and R8 to match the design. B: keep the wording and report both as met only per bag and with four people. Recommendation was A.

## Decision

- R7 is restated as "two bags of 10 kg or less, one per bearer". Result: bags of 7.2 and 8.3 kg. Met as designed.
- R8 is restated as "four people at steps, two at the lift handles". Result: about 36 kg each. Met as designed.

## Consequences

Requirements text, calculation note wording and registers only. No geometry, bill of materials or cost change. Value-engineering target: USD 1,500. Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 482 (USD 1,018 under the target).
